---
title: Implant tiers
---
The right column ranks the plan's own picks into five star tiers; an
inherited implant is ranked on its base plan. Drag a row onto another tier
to move it.

![Star tiers with implants ranked from highest to normal priority](tier-panel.png)

Tiers steer automation:

- Implants in better tiers are reserved and delivered first under the
  default iteration strategy (see [surgery options](topic:surgery-options)).
- Production queues crafting for better tiers first.

Rankings are never changed automatically. Every implant starts at three
stars and stays there until you move it.
