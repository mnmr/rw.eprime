---
title: Implant tiers
---
<!-- l10n:b-5f84f9b2d5c3 -->
The right column shows the plan's own picks in five star tiers. Implant
rankings are shared by every plan in this save: moving an implant here
changes its priority everywhere it appears. More stars mean higher priority.
Drag between star tiers or within a tier to set the order. To move an
inherited implant, open the base plan that picked it.

<!-- l10n:b-21a47f167054 -->
![Star tiers with implants ranked from highest to normal priority](tier-panel.png)

<!-- l10n:b-9a058b7e07f8 -->
Tiers steer automation:

<!-- l10n:b-cb3d9bbd27ed -->
- Implants in better tiers are reserved and delivered first under the
  default iteration strategy (see [surgery options](topic:surgery-options)).
- Production queues crafting for better tiers first.

<!-- l10n:b-4f14c0894ce2 -->
Rankings are never changed automatically. Every implant starts at three
stars and stays there until you move it.
