---
title: Creating plans
---
On the Plans tab, **New plan** asks for a name and, optionally, a plan to
extend.

![The new plan prompt with the Extends selector](newplan-dialog.png)

A plan that **extends** another inherits all of its selections. Build an
"Essentials" plan with the implants everyone should have, then extend it
into "Miner" or "Farmer" plans that add their own picks or override
inherited ones. Edit the base plan and every extending plan follows.

Each card in the plan list shows the plan's implant count, how many
colonists are enlisted, and their combined progress.

![Two plan cards with counts and progress bars](plan-cards.png)
