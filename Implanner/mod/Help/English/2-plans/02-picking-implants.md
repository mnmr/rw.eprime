---
title: Picking implants
---
The middle column lists implants discovered from vanilla, the expansions
and every mod, grouped by the body part they target.

![The implant picker with region filter and slot checkboxes](picker-tree.png)

- The **Limbs / Torso / Head** row filters the list to one body region.
- Paired parts get one entry per side: a bionic leg (left) and a bionic
  leg (right) are separate picks.
- Tick a checkbox to add the slot to the plan. Click and drag across
  checkboxes to select a whole run of slots.
- Hover any entry for its stats and market value.

Downgrades such as peg legs cannot be planned. Implants with a drawback
or a narrow use are also left out: joywire, painstopper, power claw and
pilot assistant. Implants only ghouls can take are never
listed. Purchase-only parts such as archotech implants, whose item no
workbench can craft, are hidden until **Show purchase-only implants** on
the Options tab is switched on. An archotech part the player installs
manually still counts for the slot it occupies, whether the option is on
or off. A planned purchase-only
implant never holds up the other implants in a batch, and it is installed
with the next batch once the item is in stock. A mod that adds a crafting
recipe makes the implant an ordinary one.

Some mods upgrade an installed bionic in place, like the modular bionics
from Integrated Implants. The upgraded version is listed as its own
implant. Picking it overrides the plain version in the plan. A colonist
without the plain version receives it first, then the upgrade surgery.
A colonist who already has the plain version only needs the upgrade.

![The requirement prompt for a leg module](requirement-prompt.png)

Some modded implants, like the limb modules from Bionic modularity, can
only be installed on an artificial part. Ticking one of these while the
plan has no matching replacement opens a prompt listing the replacements
that could host it. **Add to plan** adds your choice and the implant
together. **Cancel** adds nothing.
