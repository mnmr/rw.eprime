---
title: Picking implants
---
The middle column lists implants discovered from vanilla, DLC, and mods,
grouped by the body part they target.

![The implant picker with region filter and slot checkboxes](picker-tree.png)

- The **Limbs / Torso / Head** row filters the list to one body region.
- Paired parts get one entry per side: a bionic leg (left) and a bionic
  leg (right) are separate picks.
- Tick a checkbox to add the slot to the plan. Checkboxes paint: click and
  drag to select a whole run of slots.
- Hover any entry for its stats and market value.

Downgrades such as peg legs and purchase-only parts such as archotech
implants cannot be planned, though a hand-installed archotech part still
satisfies the slot it occupies.
