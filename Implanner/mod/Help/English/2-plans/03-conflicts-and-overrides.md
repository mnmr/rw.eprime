---
title: Conflicts and overrides
---
![Rows warning that selecting them overrides the planned bionic arm](picker-overrides.png)

Two implants cannot always share a body. One part holds one replacement,
some implants exclude each other outright, and some remove a rival
wherever it sits, like the mechanite strains from [FSF] Advanced Bionics
Expansion. Modules that
mount on a bionic limb, like those from Bionic modularity, share the part
with the bionic instead of replacing it. Implanner applies the same rules
as the game and resolves conflicts when you click.

- Selecting a slot that conflicts with one of the plan's own picks
  deselects the existing pick. The row warns with an "overrides" caption
  before you click. A caption too long for the row keeps its end, and
  hovering it shows the full text.
- In an extending plan, a conflicting inherited slot is suppressed
  instead: the base plan keeps its choice, your plan overrides it.

Slots covered by a base plan show an "inherited" caption. Ticking an
inherited slot adds it again as this plan's own pick.

The **Mod compatibility** options on the Options tab add two rules the
game's data cannot express; see [mod options](topic:mod-options).
