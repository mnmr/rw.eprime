---
title: Exporting plans
---
<!-- l10n:b-4d20748d1804 -->
**Export** on the Plans tab writes all of your plans, including their
implant picks and extends-links, to a small text file or the clipboard.

<!-- l10n:b-debdddaaf55e -->
- **Save** writes an .xml file to the Implanner folder (or Desktop, home
  folder, or a folder you choose).
- **Copy to clipboard** puts the same text on the clipboard, ready to
  paste into a chat message or forum post.

<!-- l10n:b-6955227f9fbd -->
Exports carry no colonists, assignments, or save data, only the plans, so
they work in any save and any colony. Implants from mods are tagged with
the mod they came from.
