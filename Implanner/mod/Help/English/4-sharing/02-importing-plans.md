---
title: Importing plans
---
**Import** on the Plans tab reads a plans export from a file or the
clipboard and shows a preview before anything happens.

- Imported plans are **added** next to your existing ones; nothing is
  replaced. A name that is already taken gets a number.
- Implants from mods you do not have are skipped; the rest of the plan is
  imported.
- A broken or unrelated file is rejected with a message and changes
  nothing.

Import a set at the start of every colony instead of rebuilding your
favorite plans by hand.
