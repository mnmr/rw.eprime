---
title: When automation stands down
---
<!-- l10n:b-a8ac20c632db -->
**Enable automation** at the top of the tab is the master switch. Switched
off, Implanner becomes a planning tool only: plans, progress, and rankings
keep working, but Implanner stops reserving items and creating crafting or
surgery bills. Existing bills you choose to keep remain active.

<!-- l10n:b-22363c7318de -->
![The automation master switch](automation-enable.png)

<!-- l10n:b-094c02cc109b -->
Switching it off first asks what to do with the bills automation already
created. Checked bills are removed. Unchecked bills remain active: colonists
can still craft or perform those operations. Implanner resumes managing any
remaining bills when automation is switched back on. Reserved items are
released either way, and Cancel keeps automation on.

<!-- l10n:b-6747bf547c20 -->
With a multi-level colony mod active (Strata, MultiFloors, As above So
below), a warning appears beside the master switch. Vanilla surgery cannot
fetch an implant across a level boundary, so an operation whose reserved
implant sits on another level waits until the implant is moved to the
patient's level. Automation keeps working within a level; keep implants,
benches and patients together to avoid the wait.
