---
title: When automation stands down
---
**Enable automation** at the top of the tab is the master switch. Switched
off, Implanner becomes a planning tool only: plans, progress, and rankings
keep working, but nothing is reserved, crafted, or scheduled.

![The automation master switch](automation-enable.png)

Switching it off first asks what to do with the bills automation already
created. Checked bills are removed. Unchecked bills stay in place and are
picked up again when automation is switched back on. Reserved items are
released either way, and Cancel keeps automation on.

With a multi-level colony mod active (Strata, MultiFloors, As above So
below), automation stands down on its own. Vanilla surgery cannot fetch an
implant across a level boundary, so rather than schedule operations that
can never complete, Implanner releases its reservations and leaves the
work to you. Bills it already created stay in place for you to keep or
cancel. Everything else keeps functioning, and implants you install by
hand still count toward progress.
