---
title: What Implanner does
---
<!-- l10n:b-8060935162d3 -->
Implanner plans colonist implants and automates the work needed to
install them.

<!-- l10n:b-7d02af1c3512 -->
- Build a **plan**: the set of implants a colonist should end up with.
- **Enlist** colonists into plans on the Overview tab.
- **Automation** crafts missing implants, reserves the items, and queues
  the operations when everything is ready.

<!-- l10n:b-64a81f113814 -->
Automation can be switched off entirely, and every step it takes shows up
in the same bills and operation lists you already use.
