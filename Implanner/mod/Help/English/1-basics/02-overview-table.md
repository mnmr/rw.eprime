---
title: The overview table
---
The Overview tab lists every colonist who could receive implants.

![Overview rows with plan, progress, priority and state columns](overview-rows.png)

- **Plan** assigns a colonist to a plan. Click for the menu, or click and
  drag down the column to paint the same plan onto several colonists.
- **Priority** decides who automation serves first, from First to Last.
  It can also be painted down the column.
- **Progress** counts installed implants against the plan.
- **State** condenses the colonist's plan status into one word.

The S and M columns show Shooting and Melee skill, and the small icons show
the equipped weapon and belt item. They are informational only; automation
ignores them.

Click a colonist's name to open their details on the right.
