<!-- l10n:summary -->
Android parts are planned per side, and android operations go to crafters
when the mod says so.

<!-- l10n:android-tiers -->
Androids have body parts of their own, such as a left and a right sight
sensor in place of eyes. The picker lists each of these parts as its own row
with its side, so you can plan both sides or just one. An implant that fits
both humans and androids, such as the VX chips, lists the brain and the
artificial brain as separate rows: tick the rows for the colonists the plan
is meant for, or make a plan for androids only, which lists android parts
alone and enlists only androids (see [creating plans](topic:creating-plans)).

<!-- l10n:surgeons -->
Android operations use the Crafting skill. With automatic doctor selection
on, Implanner restricts each android operation to the best eligible colonist
by Crafting skill, and it follows the Android Tiers setting that lets
crafters operate on androids (see [mod options](topic:mod-options)).
