<!-- l10n:summary -->
Production bills can carry a plan's minimum quality.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
When a plan sets a [minimum quality](topic:minimum-quality) above Awful and
the implant's quality is not decided by an ingredient, Implanner hands the
production bill to Quality Jobs at that target, so the bench keeps trying
until an implant good enough for the plan comes out. Without Quality Jobs a
craft below the minimum simply does not cover the slot and is crafted again.
