Mechanite strains exclude each other.

<!-- l10n:b-fc34121549e7 -->
![A picked mechanite strain in the Torso list](mechanites-picked.png)

<!-- l10n:b-8e0b09ad2325 -->
![The other strain rows showing an overrides caption naming the picked strain](mechanites-overrides.png)

<!-- l10n:b-8115e58333ac -->
Injecting one strain removes any other strain in the body, so the picker
treats every strain as one slot and the overrides caption names the strain
being replaced. Immunity-enhancing mechanites are not a strain and coexist
with every strain. Its advanced bionic bladder and advanced hygiene enhancer
are covered by the [mod compatibility options](topic:mod-options) together
with Dubs Bad Hygiene.
