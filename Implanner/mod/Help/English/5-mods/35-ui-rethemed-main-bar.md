The toolbar button appears inside the restyled bar.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Implanner draws its toolbar button through the game's own row controls,
so bar-restyling mods place it in their layout like the vanilla toggles.
Earlier versions drew it by hand and lost it behind the restyled bar.
