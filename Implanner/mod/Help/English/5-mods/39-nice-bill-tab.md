<!-- l10n:summary -->
An Implanner bench shows its production queue, and the bench checkbox has its own row.

<!-- l10n:toggle -->
On a bench that can craft implants, the **Implanner bench** checkbox sits in
its own row at the top of Nice Bill Tab's bill list, above the search field.
Benches that craft no implants show Nice Bill Tab unchanged.

<!-- l10n:vanilla-tab -->
A bench marked as an Implanner bench shows the Implanner production queue
instead of Nice Bill Tab's layout, the same tab it shows without Nice Bill
Tab (see [production options](topic:production-options)). Nice Bill Tab's
own switch and settings button are not shown on that tab. Clear the checkbox
and Nice Bill Tab's layout returns for that bench.

<!-- l10n:stray-bills -->
Nice Bill Tab can create a task on an available workbench for a missing
ingredient. Implanner benches are passed over, so the task goes to the
closest other bench that can make the item. When only Implanner benches can
make it, no task is created.
