<!-- l10n:summary -->
Operations scheduled by Implanner can be done by a surgery team.

<!-- l10n:teams -->
The **Surgery teams** option on the Options tab chooses who performs
Implanner's operations. **Solo**, the default, has one doctor operate, as
without Multi-Doctor Surgery. **Default team** uses the default surgery team
set up on Multi-Doctor Surgery's Operations tab. **Best team** has the most
skilled available doctor lead, assisted by the doctors who add the most
success chance, up to Multi-Doctor Surgery's doctor limit. Mechanoid doctors
can join the team when Multi-Doctor Surgery lists them.

<!-- l10n:rules -->
Implanner assigns the team when it schedules an operation, and the
operation then appears on Multi-Doctor Surgery's Operations tab. A team
operation waits for its lead surgeon, who may operate even when automatic
doctor selection would ask for a higher skill. When the default lead cannot
operate on that colonist, for example because the lead is the patient or is
at another colony, one doctor performs the operation without a team.
Operations excluded in Multi-Doctor Surgery's settings stay solo. Teams are
not used in multiplayer.

<!-- l10n:manual -->
Operations you add by hand still get Multi-Doctor Surgery's team dialog and
bonuses, including on colonists who follow an Implanner plan.
