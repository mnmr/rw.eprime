<!-- l10n:summary -->
Gene-granted body features do not block replacements.

<!-- l10n:gene-hediffs -->
A body feature a colonist's gene grants, such as saurid claws on the hands,
does not stop a replacement on that limb. The gene stays with the colonist,
so Implanner installs the planned bionic arm as usual. The same rule covers
other gene mods built on Vanilla Expanded Framework.
