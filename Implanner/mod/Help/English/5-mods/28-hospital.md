Hospital guests do not take up surgery slots.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
The **Count hospitalized colonists** option counts only your own
colonists and slaves in medical beds. Patients the Hospital mod brings in
never count, so a busy hospital does not hold back your colonists'
operations.
