<!-- l10n:summary -->
Races are planned by their bodies.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
A race whose body has the same parts as a human joins plans for the human
body. A race with extra parts also joins them and is offered as a body of
its own, so its extra parts can be planned. A race missing human parts gets
plans of its own (see [creating plans](topic:creating-plans)). A race whose
body nests organs differently does not change what other colonists' plans
allow.
