<!-- l10n:summary -->
Its implants mount on a bionic but never under one.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Combat Bionics installs an implant such as the swarm wrist on top of a
replacement at the same body part, but the game never keeps it under a
bionic above it. Implanner follows that rule: the swarm wrist can be planned
beside a bionic hand but not beside a bionic arm.
