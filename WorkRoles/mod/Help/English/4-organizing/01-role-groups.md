---
title: Role groups
---
The role tree keeps your roster in named, collapsible groups:

![A role group with nested roles](role-tree.png)

Click a group header to collapse or expand it. The editor's Group button
moves the selected role by menu, and its New option creates a fresh
group; [dragging](topic:role-tree) in the tree does the same by hand.

Groups are pure organization: they never affect what anyone works on.
