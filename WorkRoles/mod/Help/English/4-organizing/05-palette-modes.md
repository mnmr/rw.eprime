---
title: Palette arrangement
---
The role palette on the Colonists tab can be arranged two ways, chosen at
the bottom of the Options tab's display settings:

![Display settings with the palette switch](options-display.png)

Skills mode clusters roles by the skill they lean on (the default, shown
throughout this manual). Groups mode mirrors your own
[role groups](topic:role-groups) instead, in the order you keep them.

A per-player choice, like everything on that panel.
