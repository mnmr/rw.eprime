---
title: Nested and flat views
---
The Display toggle above the tree switches nesting:

![The nested toggle](nested-toggle.png)

Nested view shows [auto-nesting](topic:role-tree), with covered roles
indented under the role covering them. A dashed swatch marks a role
shown under a parent while belonging to another group; the tooltip names
its real home.

Flat view lists every role exactly where its group puts it, nothing
inferred. Both views show the same roles.
