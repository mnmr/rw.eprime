---
title: Search and job filter
---
<!-- l10n:b-3f00612303ce -->
Two controls above the tree find roles fast. The search box matches role
names as you type. The job filter lists only roles containing the chosen
job, which answers the eternal question "who on earth is allowed to
slaughter the alpacas".

<!-- l10n:b-39909f3def26 -->
Both only filter the view; selections and assignments stay untouched.
