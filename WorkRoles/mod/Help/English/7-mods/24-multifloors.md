<!-- l10n:summary -->
Floors on the same tile count as one location.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
A location-bound role covers every floor of the colony, and the colonist
table lists the colonists of all floors together.

<!-- l10n:scanner -->
MultiFloors issues work through its own prioritized scanner. WorkRoles
makes that scanner follow the role job lists, so a colonist never receives a
job the roles did not give them.
