<!-- l10n:summary -->
A mech in smart work mode counts as working.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
A mech counts as working in any work mode that actually runs work, so
Smart Work Mode's smart work keeps the mech in coverage and
recommendations. Escort, combat, recharge and self-shutdown modes take a
mech out of both.
