<!-- l10n:summary -->
PriorityMaster changes manual priority numbers and their range.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
WorkRoles replaces those controls and derives managed colonists' priorities
from roles, so PriorityMaster's writes are blocked. When that happens a
one-time notice names the mod and suggests the role-based way to get the
same effect.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Role [ordering](topic:ordering) decides what a
colonist does first.
