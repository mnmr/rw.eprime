<!-- l10n:summary -->
Robots follow their Work tab, and WorkRoles reads what each model can do
from it.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
With this add-on a Misc. Robots robot picks work the way a colonist does,
from Work tab priorities, and every work type its model declares counts as
possible whatever its declared priority. WorkRoles uses that rule for
capability checks, recommendations and role chips, so a Misc. Robots++
Crafter or Omni bot can research and every model can fight fires through
its roles instead of showing a critical chip.
