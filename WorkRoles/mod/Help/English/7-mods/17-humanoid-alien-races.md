<!-- l10n:summary -->
Race work restrictions are read from the framework itself.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
For audited Humanoid Alien Races builds on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7,
recommendations use the framework's actual work-giver and recipe
restrictions, so every race using those rules benefits. A blocked recipe
does not remove other usable recipes from the role or count as a way to
train its skill.

<!-- l10n:limits -->
Unknown framework builds keep existing eligibility and report that their
rules are unsupported. Restrictions hidden inside arbitrary jobs cannot be
detected safely. Unverified race-specific rules, such as Ratkin's, keep the
existing behaviour; no race or xenotype name is used to guess a restriction.
