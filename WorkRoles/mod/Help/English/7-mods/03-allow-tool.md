Allow Tool's jobs slot into the matching seeded roles on their own.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Haul+ joins Basics and the other jobs join the roles that already cover
their work. One-off jobs such as Finish Off do not make Basics or Hunter
count as melee roles. Adding WorkRoles to a save that already uses Allow
Tool keeps Haul+ assigned to the colonists who had it.
