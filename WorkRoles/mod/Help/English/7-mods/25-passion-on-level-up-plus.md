Works, but recommendations should lean less on passions.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
With passions that change as skills level up, the current passion says
less about a colonist. Under [Global tuning](topic:global-tuning), lower
the passion signal multipliers in Champion selection (or raise its lowest
signal to A) and drop the passion weights in Final role ordering, so
recommendations follow skill levels instead.
