<!-- l10n:summary -->
Vanilla Skills Expanded expertise feeds the recommendation signals.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
An expertise shows in the colonist stats panel and outranks a burning
passion in the recommendation engine. Vanilla Skills Expanded can also
supply job profiles that say which skills a job uses and trains; WorkRoles
leaves job eligibility and execution to the mod itself.
