<!-- l10n:summary -->
Personal Work Categories sets work priorities itself.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
Any mod that sets priorities fights WorkRoles over the same data, since
setting priorities is what WorkRoles is for.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Custom roles with your own job selection cover the
same ground (see [Picking jobs](topic:picking-jobs)).
