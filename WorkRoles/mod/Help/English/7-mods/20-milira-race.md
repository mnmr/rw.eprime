<!-- l10n:summary -->
Milira colonists take roles; Milian androids are mechs and take roles with
robot and mech support on.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Milira are ordinary colonists to WorkRoles. Milian androids are Biotech
mechanoids with an overseer, so with robot and mech support enabled they
appear in Colonists, get recommendations and follow their roles like any
mech. Their Milian work tab keeps managing Milians with no roles; for a
Milian with roles its priority writes are blocked with a one-time notice,
like Mech Work Tab.
