<!-- l10n:summary -->
Complex Jobs does what WorkRoles does, with a different interface.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
Both mods let you set priorities for jobs the vanilla Work tab cannot
reach: Complex Jobs by expanding the grid with jobs, WorkRoles by creating,
assigning and ordering roles. Its split work types appear in the role
editor as separate jobs that no seeded role or recommendation covers.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Roles can hold any single job, so the role editor
already gives the same control (see [Picking jobs](topic:picking-jobs)).
