<!-- l10n:summary -->
Mech Work Tab keeps managing any mech that has no WorkRoles assignment.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Mech priority mods such as Mech Work Tab can manage workers with no WorkRoles
assignments. If you enable robot and mech support, WorkRoles owns work ordering
for mechanical workers with assigned roles too, and the tab's priority writes
for those mechs are blocked with a one-time notice. Turn support off in Options
to restore native ordering for all of them; their saved roles remain dormant.
