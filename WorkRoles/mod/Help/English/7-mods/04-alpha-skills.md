Alpha Skills expertise feeds the recommendation signals.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
An expertise shows in the colonist stats panel and outranks a burning
passion in the recommendation engine. Alpha Skills can also supply job
profiles that say which skills a job uses and trains; WorkRoles leaves job
eligibility and execution to the mod itself.
