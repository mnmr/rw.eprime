<!-- l10n:summary -->
Gathering recommendations account for gathering tools.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Supported Vivi versions provide equipment-dependent gathering speed, yield
and eligibility, so a Vivi with the right tool is recommended for the
gathering it can actually do. Swapping tools updates the recommendation
at once, even while paused.

<!-- l10n:limits -->
Unknown Vivi builds and custom gathering rules keep the existing behaviour.
