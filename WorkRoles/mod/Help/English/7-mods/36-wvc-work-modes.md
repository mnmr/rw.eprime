<!-- l10n:summary -->
A mech in any of its work modes counts as working.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
A mech counts as working in any work mode that actually runs work, so the
work modes WVC adds keep the mech in coverage and recommendations. Its
combat, escort and recharge modes take a mech out of both. Its mech work
tab opens beside WorkRoles; see [Mech Work Tab](topic:mech-work-tab) for
how priority writes from such tabs are handled.
