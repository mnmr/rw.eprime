<!-- l10n:summary -->
Finishing work that Quality Jobs assigns stays inside the roles.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
The work Quality Jobs hands to its chosen finisher is recognised as role
work, so the colonist table does not report it as a job outside every role.
When robot and mech support is on, mechanical recommendations follow the
Quality Jobs option that lets mechs and robots prepare managed work.
Role assignments can organise who finishes; the finished item's work type
must still be enabled for the colonist.
