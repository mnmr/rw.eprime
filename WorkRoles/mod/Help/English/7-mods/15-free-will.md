<!-- l10n:summary -->
Free Will sets colonists' work priorities itself.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
WorkRoles owns priorities for managed colonists, so Free Will's writes are
blocked. When that happens a one-time notice names the mod and suggests the
role-based way to get the same effect.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Recommendations and [Auto-optimize](topic:auto-optimize)
cover the same ground with roles.
