<!-- l10n:summary -->
Enhanced Work Tab replaces the Work tab and controls priorities.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
Work tab replacements fight with WorkRoles over the same UI and the same
priorities, so the two cannot both manage a colony.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Roles replace the per-colonist priority grid, and the
[priority grid](topic:priority-grid) remains available for a manual look.
