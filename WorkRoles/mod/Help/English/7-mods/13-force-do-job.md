<!-- l10n:summary -->
Force Do Job sets work priorities itself.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
Its priority writes are blocked for managed colonists, so the forced job
never takes effect and the "not assigned to" notice keeps appearing.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. For urgent work, copy a role such as Hauler, give the
copy to colonists at the front of their assignments and toggle it on when
needed (see [States](topic:states)).
