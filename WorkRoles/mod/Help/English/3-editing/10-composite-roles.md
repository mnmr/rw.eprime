---
title: Composite roles
---
<!-- l10n:b-a3d921635f8f -->
A composite role bundles whole roles instead of jobs. Flip the Composite
switch and the job panes become role panes:

<!-- l10n:b-cfbaf84e93d6 -->
![Member roles of a composite](member-roles.png)

<!-- l10n:b-65ba63a66d81 -->
Holders do the member roles' jobs in the member order, as one assignment.
A composite bundling Farmer and Handler gives you a single chip for the
full outdoor package.

<!-- l10n:b-d1d6142f5648 -->
House rules: members must be regular roles without hour or location
rules, and a composite cannot contain another composite. Editing a member
role updates every composite bundling it.

<!-- l10n:b-842cedf95cbc -->
Several rows in the Member Roles list can be selected, moved and removed
at once, the same way as in the [Selected Jobs list](topic:picking-jobs).
