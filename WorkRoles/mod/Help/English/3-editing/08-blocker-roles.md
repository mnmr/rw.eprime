---
title: Blocker roles
---
<!-- l10n:b-2cf92d190e02 -->
A blocker turns a role inside out: its jobs become forbidden instead of
allowed.

<!-- l10n:b-925acf6aec59 -->
![The role type switches with Blocker on](blocker-toggles.png)

<!-- l10n:b-fe568baeb7aa -->
Everything after the blocker in a colonist's row is affected; roles
placed before it still win. The seeded Pyrophobe role holds only
Firefighting: put it before Core and that colonist never fights fires,
put a Firefighter role before it to make an exception.

<!-- l10n:b-b5efb789701b -->
Colonists genetically terrified of fire receive Pyrophobe automatically,
placed where it belongs.
