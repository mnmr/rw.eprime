---
title: Role tree
---
The left side of the Roles tab lists every role in the colony. Most of
it is your own named groups, like this one:

![The Indoors group with nested roles](role-tree.png)

Groups have no editor screen because they need none: assign a role to a
new group name and the group appears, move its last role away and it
vanishes. Drag roles to reorder them inside a group or to move them
between groups; click a role to open it in the
[editor](topic:role-editor).

The indentation above is auto-nesting: a role whose jobs fully cover
another shows the covered role indented beneath it, so Handyman sits
under Builder, and Fix Roof under Handyman. A covered role can appear
under several parents; the repeated appearances are extra listings of
the same role, and its own group remains the real home.

Above the groups sit the display controls, covered under
[Organizing](topic:tree-views):

![Search, display and job filter](tree-header.png)

And first among the groups gathers everything rule-driven:

![The automatic conditional group](tree-conditional.png)

[Conditional roles](topic:conditional-roles) collect here automatically
and return to their stored group when their rules are cleared.
