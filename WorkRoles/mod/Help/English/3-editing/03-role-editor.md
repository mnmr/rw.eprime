---
title: Role editor
---
Selecting a role opens it in the editor:

![The editor header](role-editor-header.png)

For a normal role the header covers the everyday settings: rename beside
the name, [icon and color](topic:icons-colors), the
[group](topic:role-groups) the role lives in, and the age groups allowed
to hold it. Roles whose jobs use skills also list them here, derived
automatically from the [selected jobs](topic:picking-jobs).

The switches on the right turn a role into one of the special
[role types](topic:role-types); for a normal role they all stay off.
