---
title: Conditional roles
---
A conditional role switches itself on and off by rules:

![Active hours and location rules](role-rules.png)

The hour strip sets when the role applies (paint hours off to carve out
downtime), and the location picker sets where: settlements, caravans, or
a named ship. A role becomes conditional the moment any rule is set, and
gathers under the automatic Conditional Roles group in the tree.

While a rule suppresses the role, its chips dim and their tooltips say
why ("Off: outside active hours"). The seeded setup has none of these;
the classic homemade example is a mining role that only applies while the
colonist is away from home.
