---
title: Icons and colors
---
Click the icon square beside the role's name to pick a new one:

![The icon picker](icon-picker.png)

Colors come from the palette in the editor's top-right corner:

![The color palette](editor-color-palette.png)

The left block holds mild built-ins, the bright grid is the shared
palette, and the plus row underneath stores custom colors. Click an empty
plus to define one, right-click a filled slot to redefine it. Custom
colors are shared across all roles, so a recolor updates every role using
that slot.
