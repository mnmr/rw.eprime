---
title: Jobs from other mods
---
<!-- l10n:b-255ade45f846 -->
Work added by other mods shows up in the job tree automatically, no
compatibility patch required.

<!-- l10n:b-c529438846ab -->
When new work appears mid-game, WorkRoles extends or creates roles to
cover it and posts a message naming what changed. Jobs from AllowTool and
Keyz' Allow Utilities slot into the matching seeded roles on their own.

<!-- l10n:b-d07aeb35d96c -->
If a job ends up in no role at all, the editor shows an amber warning so
nothing silently goes undone. And when a mod leaves your game, its jobs
go dormant inside their roles until it returns or the job is manually
removed from the role definition.
