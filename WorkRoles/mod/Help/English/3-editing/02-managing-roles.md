---
title: Creating and deleting roles
---
The buttons under the role tree manage the roster:

![New, Copy and Delete buttons](manage-buttons.png)

New starts an empty role, Copy duplicates the selected one (a good
starting point for a variant), and Delete removes it from every colonist
after a confirmation. Every seeded role can be edited, renamed, or
deleted; [Restore Defaults](topic:restore-defaults) brings any of them
back later.

Rename from the editor header next to the role's name. Names must be
unique, mostly so future-you knows which "Hauler 2" was the good one.
