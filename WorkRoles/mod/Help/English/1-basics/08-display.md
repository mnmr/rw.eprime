## Chip layout

The button beside Display switches between two layouts. Its face shows
the active one: a brick wall for chips stacked at their natural widths,
or a grid for chips aligned in equal-width columns.

Initials in grid columns:

![Rows with initials aligned in grid columns](initials-grid-row.png)

Icons in grid columns:

![Rows with icons aligned in grid columns](icons-grid-row.png)

Full-name grid columns are sized for eight leading letters, and a
longer name fades out at the edge of its chip. A name gets more room
only when it needs it to stay distinct from a similar role name. The
chip tooltip always shows the whole name. Right-click the layout button
to choose Short, Medium or Long instead, which size every column the
same, or Full to show every name whole.

## Grouping

Group by splits the table into collapsible sections, here by gender:

![The table grouped by gender](grouped-table.png)

Grouping only arranges rows; the filters still decide who is listed, and
with Colony Groups installed its pawn groups appear as an option.

## Skill columns

The Skills button adds up to three skill columns to the table:

![A skill column with sorted values](skill-column.png)

Click a column header to sort the table by that skill, click its X to
remove the column. Values carry the same passion flames and suitability
colors as the colonist panel.

## Options tab

More display switches live on the Options tab:

![Display settings on the Options tab](options-display.png)

All of these are personal preferences: in multiplayer each player keeps
their own, and nothing here changes any assignment.
