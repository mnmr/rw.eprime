---
title: Priority grid
---
Missing the old numbers? The two small numbered buttons
![the priority grid buttons](priority-grid-buttons.png) above the
table's scrollbar open a read-only priority grid, one for each view:

![The priority grid](priority-grid.png)

The grid shows the priorities WorkRoles computed for every listed
colonist, respecting the active filters. The button at the top switches
between the mod's raw ranks and the familiar vanilla 0 to 4 view. Drag
the corner grip to resize; double-click it to snap back.

Two related switches live on the Options tab:

![Compatibility settings](options-compat.png)

The first is vanilla's own "manual priorities" toggle (it lives here
because WorkRoles replaced the tab that hosted it). The second maps the
mod's sequential priorities onto the vanilla 0 to 4 range for other mods
that read them.
