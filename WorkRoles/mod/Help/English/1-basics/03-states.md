---
title: Role states
---
A role can be turned off globally (for the whole colony) and/or per
colonist.

Clicking a role in the palette toggles it globally, from
![palette chip, enabled](palette-chip-on.png) to
![palette chip, disabled](palette-chip-off.png).

While a role is disabled globally, its chip wears a double strike
![chip with a double strike](chip-state-global-off.png) on every colonist
holding it.

Clicking a chip in a colonist's row cycles between on
![enabled chip](chip-state-on.png), off for this colonist
![chip with a single strike](chip-state-off.png), and forced on
![chip with a claim flag](chip-state-forced.png). Off both globally and
for the colonist stacks the strikes
![chip with stacked strikes](chip-state-both-off.png), while forced on
keeps working even when the role is disabled globally.

Toggling never removes the assignment; the chip keeps its place in the
row.

Handy for roles you keep parked: hand Plant Cutter to half the colony,
leave it off globally, and flip one palette chip the day the blight
arrives.

Unsure about a chip? Its tooltip names the state in the framed corner:

![The role chip tooltip with the state corner framed](chip-tooltip-state.png)
