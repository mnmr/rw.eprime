---
title: Assigning roles
---
Roles live in the palette at the top of the Colonists tab, clustered by
the skill they depend on, plus Everyone and Unskilled clusters for the
universal stuff:

![The left part of the role palette](palette-left.png)

Every palette chip tells you how to use it when hovered:

![Palette chip tooltip](palette-tooltip.png)

Three ways to hand out a role:

- Drag a palette chip onto a colonist's row.
- Shift-click a palette chip to assign it to the selected colonists.
- Click the ![plus button](tex:UI/Buttons/Plus|24) at the end of a
colonist's row and pick from the menu.

Dropping a chip also picks its position in the row, so a drag assigns and
prioritizes in one motion. The tooltip's Best fits list is a preview of
who the [recommendation engine](topic:recommendations) would give it to.

To hand a role to several colonists at once, select them first: click a
name, Shift-click another name to select the range between them,
Ctrl-click to add or remove single colonists, or press Ctrl+A to select
everyone in the table:

![Three selected colonists](selected-rows.png)

A modified click on a palette chip then assigns the role to all of them
and decides where it lands in each row:

- Shift-click puts it last.
- Ctrl+Shift-click puts it right after Basics.
- Alt-click puts it where the [recommendation engine](topic:recommendations)
ranks it for that colonist.

While several colonists are selected the bottom panel is hidden. Click a
single colonist to bring it back.
