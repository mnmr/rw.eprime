<!-- l10n:b-3f88926bfefb -->
Roles live in the palette at the top of the Colonists tab, clustered by
the skill they depend on, plus Everyone and Unskilled clusters for the
universal stuff:

<!-- l10n:b-7936455c781b -->
![The left part of the role palette](palette-left.png)

<!-- l10n:b-d32372287bff -->
Every palette chip tells you how to use it when hovered:

<!-- l10n:b-a73595aee39c -->
![Palette chip tooltip](palette-tooltip.png)

<!-- l10n:b-7e67f671af04 -->
Three ways to hand out a role:

<!-- l10n:b-33a2b6afaa0a -->
- Drag a palette chip onto a colonist's row.
- Shift-click a palette chip to assign it to the selected colonist, or
to every selected colonist (see below).
- Click the ![plus button](tex:UI/Buttons/Plus|24) at the end of a
colonist's row and pick from the menu.

<!-- l10n:b-3634ad4cacb9 -->
Dropping a chip also picks its position in the row, so a drag assigns and
prioritizes in one motion. The tooltip's Best fits list is a preview of
who the [recommendation engine](topic:recommendations) would give it to.

<!-- l10n:b-e85c035e0a4a -->
## Several colonists at once

<!-- l10n:b-5f1d83c001e3 -->
Select the colonists first. Click a name to select one, Shift-click
another name to select everyone between the two, and Ctrl-click to add
or remove single colonists. Ctrl+A selects everyone listed in the table:

<!-- l10n:b-9d1cb80ee3d7 -->
![Three selected colonists](selected-rows.png)

<!-- l10n:b-04bce3f932d8 -->
Clicking a palette chip with a modifier key held assigns the role to all
of them. The modifier decides where the role lands in each row:

<!-- l10n:b-0d670603dcf1 -->
- Shift-click puts it last.
- Ctrl+Shift-click puts it right after Basics.
- Alt-click puts it where the [recommendation engine](topic:recommendations)
would rank it for that colonist.

<!-- l10n:b-ca2be739e738 -->
Colonists who already hold the role are skipped. While several colonists
are selected the [colonist panel](topic:selected-panel) is hidden; click
a single colonist to bring it back.
