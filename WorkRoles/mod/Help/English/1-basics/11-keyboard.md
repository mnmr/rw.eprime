---
title: Keyboard navigation
---
The colonist table reads the keyboard while no text field has focus:

- Up and Down arrows select the previous or next colonist. Hold Shift to
extend the selection instead.
- Home and End jump to the first or last. Hold Ctrl to ignore group
sections.
- Page Up and Page Down move a page (or group) at a time.
- Ctrl+A selects every colonist listed in the table, collapsed groups
included.
- Ctrl+C copies the selected colonist's role row and Ctrl+V pastes it,
the same as the [copy and paste buttons](topic:copy-paste).
- Ctrl+F puts the cursor in the search box.

The six movement bindings live under WorkRoles in the game's keyboard
configuration, ready to be remapped.
