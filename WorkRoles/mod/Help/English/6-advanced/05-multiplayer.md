<!-- l10n:b-18974b84b4d6 -->
WorkRoles is fully RimWorld Multiplayer compatible. Every change that
matters (role edits, assignments, toggles, pins, tuning) travels as a
synced command, so all players stay in perfect agreement about who does
what.

<!-- l10n:b-e29b9d1f2b8c -->
Presentation stays personal: display modes, verdict stars, and groupings
are yours alone and never touch the shared state.
