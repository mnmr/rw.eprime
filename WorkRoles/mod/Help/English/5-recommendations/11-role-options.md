---
title: Role options
---
Expand a role under Role Options to tune how the engine treats it:

![Smith's role options expanded](role-options-expanded.png)

- **Colony importance**: Important promotes the role above same-strength
production work in a colonist's row, Optional pushes it toward the end.
- **Time demand**: part-time duties sit above full-time work of equal
strength, opportunistic filler drops last.
- **Repeat champion penalty**: discourages one overachiever (the
engine's champion pick) from anchoring every role; switch it off for
roles where that is fine.
- **Assignment Scaling**: the colony minimum is how many holders the
engine must find, coverage is the percentage of colonists it should aim
for.
- **Skills**: work types and used, trained, and gated skills are derived
from the role's jobs. Add Skill adds a hard gate: a colonist with that
skill disabled is never recommended the role.
- **Training Path**: covered in [its own topic](topic:training-paths).

The seeded roles come pre-tuned; these options are how you disagree.
