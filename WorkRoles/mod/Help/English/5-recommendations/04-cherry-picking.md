title: Applying single roles
---
<!-- l10n:b-83e1d42e34a4 -->
The Recommended Roles list in the colonist panel shows the engine's
proposed row for the selected colonist:

<!-- l10n:b-d952526f1859 -->
![The Recommended Roles list](recommended-roles.png)

<!-- l10n:b-4ad9f51efc1b -->
Click any chip to assign just that role, exactly where the engine would
place it. The panel mixes three kinds of chip: a normal chip is a role
the engine wants to add, a dimmed chip is already assigned (clicking it
changes nothing), and a red border marks a current role the engine would
remove.

<!-- l10n:b-a16f70b7ebec -->
Cherry-picking is the gentlest way to use the engine: take the ideas you
like and ignore the rest, nothing needs [pinning](topic:pinning) first.
