---
title: Reading the reasoning
---
No verdict is a black box. Hover a skill in the colonist panel and the
tooltip lays out every signal behind the rating:

![The signal breakdown tooltip](skill-tooltip.png)

The Source column is worth a glance when another mod's genes or skills
are in play. Chips in the Recommended Roles list explain themselves the
same way: their tooltips state why the role was picked, whether it fills
a colony minimum, arrived through a training path, or scored on signals
alone.
