---
title: Training paths
---
A training path describes how colonists grow into a role. Caretaker's
path stages the social duties:

![Caretaker's training path with overlapping bands](training-path.png)

Each band is a role with the skill range where it applies, and bands may
overlap: here Jailor covers Social 0 to 4, Warden takes over from 4, and
Caretaker joins from 6 without replacing Warden. A colonist at Social 2
is recommended Jailor, at 5 Warden, and at 10 both Warden and Caretaker.

Drag a band's ends to resize its range, drag the middle to move it.
Where bands do not overlap the later role replaces the earlier; where
they do, the roles complement. Roles that train none of the target's
skills warn before being added; you can still add one when a mod changes
what grants XP.
