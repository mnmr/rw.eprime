---
title: Pinning assignments
---
<!-- l10n:b-1075eaae5085 -->
Right-click a chip and pick Pin. A pinned chip wears a pin marker:

<!-- l10n:b-62dc1c2c1729 -->
![A pinned role chip](chip-pinned.png)

<!-- l10n:b-25aab1d3dbee -->
Pinned assignments are off limits to the recommendation engine:
[Make It So](topic:make-it-so), [Fix My Colony](topic:fix-my-colony), and
[Auto-optimize](topic:auto-optimize) preserve them, even when their role or a
composite member is in [Excluded roles](topic:recommendation-order).
Pin the assignments you set by hand and let the engine argue about the
rest.

<!-- l10n:b-ff2cacf8d737 -->
[Conditional roles](topic:conditional-roles) need no pin: rule-driven
assignments are left alone, along with blockers, unless excluded.
[What is never touched](topic:untouched) has the full list.
