---
title: Global options
---
The right side of the Recommendations tab holds the engine's dials,
folded into five sections. Most players will never need to tune these
settings, but the knobs are available to those that would like to tweak
the recommendations.

![The five global option sections](global-sections.png)

- **Champion selection** decides who anchors each role.
- **Training path and optional target** options govern when paths
activate and when optional multi-skill roles are worth recommending.
- **Lead diversification** spreads training leads across colonists.
- **Final role ordering** decides how a colonist's recommended roles are
sequenced.
- **Hunter placement** sets the shooting cutoffs for Hunter's dynamic
slot.

Every dial explains itself in place, and each section's Reset restores
the defaults after adventurous experiments.
