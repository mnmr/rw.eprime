# WorkRoles ideas

Possible follow-ups, not scheduled. Each entry records the problem it would address and the options considered so a later task can start from the decision, not the investigation.

## Partial-role markers on mechs and robots (2026-10-30)

Every shipped role is written for colonists, so vanilla mechs and some robots are recommended roles that contain jobs they cannot run: Core's two patient jobs on every medical mech, six hauling jobs on the Lifter's Hauler, Drill on the Tunneler's Miner, tree jobs on the Agrihand's Farmer, brewing/subcore/research extras on the Fabricor, animal and self-care jobs on the Paramedic's Doctor. Compiled job orders already exclude those jobs, so the work is correct; the chip shows the Caution marker. Its tooltip names the blocked jobs when there are three or fewer and otherwise says "N of the M jobs in this role" with the reason, so the Lifter, Paramedic and Fabricor cases do not list job names today.

Options, usable alone or together:

1. **Seed mech-specific roles.** When mechanical support is on, offer a button (Options or Restore defaults) that creates tailored roles from the installed models' native work (e.g. "Lifter", "Paramedic", "ER Bot") so robots get exact-fit roles without markers. Normal roles stay eligible where they fit; the planner's closest-fit rule would prefer the tailored role automatically.
2. **Option to suppress the marker.** A per-save or per-player setting that hides capability markers for mechanical workers, or for everyone, for players who do not want them. Keep tooltips available so the information is still reachable.
3. **List all blocked jobs for mechanical workers.** Raise or remove the three-job cap in the capability tooltip for mechs and robots, since "not supported by this model" is a permanent fact worth naming in full.
