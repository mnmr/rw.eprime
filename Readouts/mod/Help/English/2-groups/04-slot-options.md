---
title: Slot options
---
Click a slot in the Group Editor to open the options for that resource or resource pool (see [What a resource pool is](topic:what-a-resource-pool-is)). The options for the Meals slot:

![Options for the Meals slot](slot-options.png)

- Show when zero keeps the slot visible when the count is zero. Turn it off and the slot disappears until you own some again. A slot with a threshold, or with a negative count, always stays visible.
- Low and Critical set the thresholds that color the counter. Enter the numbers and click Set; Clear removes them. The counter turns pale yellow at or below Low and orange at or below Critical, so Critical should be the smaller number.
- The two rows at the bottom override the count options for this resource or pool. Default follows the Options tab, while Always On and Always Off ignore it (see [Count options](topic:count-options)).

Thresholds and count overrides belong to the resource or pool, so the same resource shows the same colors and counts in every group. They are saved with the game and apply to every player. Show when zero belongs to the slot.
