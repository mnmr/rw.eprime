<!-- l10n:b-fff1c1b27075 -->
The Groups panel lists every group in the order the readout shows them:

<!-- l10n:b-77cfd61d39be -->
![The Groups panel](groups-panel.png)

<!-- l10n:b-baedb8307388 -->
- Click a name to open the group in the Group Editor.
- Click the checkmark to show or hide the group in the readout. This is your own view; other players in a multiplayer game keep theirs.
- Click the X to delete the group. A confirmation prompt appears first.
- Drag a name up or down to change the order of the bands.
- Type a name in the field at the bottom and click Add to create a group. The new group opens in the Group Editor and is turned on for you.

<!-- l10n:b-89b87ecc2245 -->
The group name and a rename button sit above the tiers in the Group Editor.
