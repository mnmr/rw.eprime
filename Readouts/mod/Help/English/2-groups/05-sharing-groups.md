---
title: Sharing groups
---
The three buttons at the top right of the Overview tab share or reset your setup:

![Import, Export and Restore defaults](editor-buttons.png)

Export writes your pools and groups, including each slot's Show when zero setting, to a file or to the clipboard. Thresholds and count overrides are not exported.

Import reads such a file or the clipboard and replaces your pools and groups. Thresholds and count overrides are cleared as well, and entries that need a mod you do not have are skipped. Restore defaults replaces everything with the set that ships with the mod.

Import shows a preview and a warning before it changes anything, and Restore defaults shows a confirmation prompt. After either one, every group starts with one tier and its default visibility again. In a multiplayer game both change the groups for every player.
