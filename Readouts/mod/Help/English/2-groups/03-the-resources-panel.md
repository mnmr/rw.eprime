title: The Resources panel
---
<!-- l10n:b-0e7ec78d41dc -->
The Resources panel on the right lists everything you can put in a group, sorted into the game's own categories. Three filters at its top narrow the list:

<!-- l10n:b-4b4f375fc92f -->
![The filters at the top of the Resources panel](resources-filters.png)

<!-- l10n:b-f050df826acd -->
- The search filter matches resource names and category names. A matching category shows all of its contents.
- The item filter switches between Resources, the items the game's own readout shows, and All Storable Items, which adds everything that can sit in a stockpile.
- The source filter limits the list to one mod, or to the base game and its expansions under Vanilla.

<!-- l10n:b-5db26dc68065 -->
The filters are shared with the Resource Pool Editor and reset when the editor window closes. The item filter opens as a menu:

<!-- l10n:b-daf69a525610 -->
![The item filter choices](item-filter-menu.png)

<!-- l10n:b-17833111f74d -->
A green checkmark marks an entry that is already in the selected group. Click the checkmark, or right-click the entry, to remove it from the group:

<!-- l10n:b-c52dfcefd411 -->
![The Foods category with items that are already in the group](resources-tree.png)
