---
title: Tiers in the Group Editor
---
The Group Editor shows one band per tier, with the same icons and counts as the readout:

![The Food group in the Group Editor](group-tiers.png)

A group starts with one tier, and a new empty tier appears below as soon as the tier above it holds something. Each tier holds up to eight slots, and a resource can appear only once per group.

- Click a resource in the Resources panel to append it to the last tier, or to a new tier when that one is full.
- Drag a resource from the Resources panel onto a slot to place it there.
- Drag a slot to move it within a tier or to another tier.
- Right-click a slot to remove it from the group. A tier that ends up empty disappears, and the tiers below it move up.
- Click a slot to open its options (see [Slot options](topic:slot-options)).

The tiers belong to the save, so every player in a multiplayer game sees the same groups.
