---
title: Hovering and clicking a slot
---
Hold the mouse over an icon to see the name, the exact count, and the description behind the counter. For a resource pool (see [What a resource pool is](topic:what-a-resource-pool-is)) the tooltip lists every member you own some of, with its own count:

![The tooltip for the Meals pool](slot-tooltip.png)

Click an icon or its counter to select the matching stacks on the map. Hold Shift to add them to the current selection, or to deselect them when they are all selected already. The selection follows the count options on the Options tab (see [Count options](topic:count-options)) and stops at the game's limit of 200 selected items.

With the click option "Jump camera to clicked resource" the camera also moves to the closest of the stacks the click selected (see [Click options](topic:click-options)).
