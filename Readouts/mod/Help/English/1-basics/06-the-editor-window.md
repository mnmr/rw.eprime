---
title: The editor window
---
<!-- l10n:b-f9719aa65c62 -->
Click the gear button at the top left of the readout panel to open the editor. The Overview tab is where you build groups and pools:

<!-- l10n:b-2193f3a9112b -->
![The Overview tab with the Food group selected](editor-window.png)

<!-- l10n:b-beeef5679bfe -->
- The tabs at the top switch between Overview, Options, and Help. Options holds the panel's display, counting, position and size settings, and Help is this manual.
- The Groups panel on the left lists your groups and selects the one you edit (see [The groups list](topic:the-groups-list)).
- The middle panel is the Group Editor for the selected group. The two buttons at its top switch it to the Resource Pool Editor (see [Tiers in the Group Editor](topic:tiers-in-the-group-editor) and [Editing a pool](topic:editing-a-pool)).
- The Resources panel on the right lists everything you can add to a group or a pool (see [The Resources panel](topic:the-resources-panel)).
- The Help row at the top of each panel folds a short description in or out.

<!-- l10n:b-cf4e57aa6590 -->
The editor can stay open while the game runs, and every change applies at once. There is no Apply or Cancel button.

<!-- l10n:b-53028abc0ad7 -->
The four buttons at the top are Import, Export, Set defaults, and Restore defaults. Import and Export share a setup, Set defaults saves a personal setup for future colonies, and Restore defaults resets the current colony (see [Sharing groups](topic:sharing-groups) and [New-game defaults](topic:new-game-defaults)).

<!-- l10n:b-b63d50023980 -->
You can also open the Options tab through Options → Mod Options → EPrime's Readouts, including from the main menu. Both routes use the same settings window (see [Panel position and size](topic:panel-position-and-size)).
