---
title: The editor window
---
Click the gear button at the top left of the readout panel to open the editor. It opens on the Overview tab:

![The Overview tab with the Food group selected](editor-window.png)

- The tabs at the top switch between Overview, Options, and Help. Options holds the display options for the panel, and Help is this manual.
- The Groups panel on the left lists your groups and selects the one you edit (see [The groups list](topic:the-groups-list)).
- The middle panel is the Group Editor for the selected group. The two buttons at its top switch it to the Resource Pool Editor (see [Tiers in the Group Editor](topic:tiers-in-the-group-editor) and [Editing a pool](topic:editing-a-pool)).
- The Resources panel on the right lists everything you can add to a group or a pool (see [The Resources panel](topic:the-resources-panel)).
- The Help row at the top of each panel folds a short description in or out.

The editor can stay open while the game runs, and every change applies at once. There is no Apply or Cancel button.

The buttons at the top right of the Overview tab export, import, or reset your whole setup (see [Sharing groups](topic:sharing-groups)).
