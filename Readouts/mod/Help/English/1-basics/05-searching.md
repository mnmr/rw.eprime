---
title: Searching
---
Type at least two characters in the search field at the top of the panel to find any resource, whether it sits in a group or not:

![Search results for "meal"](search-results.png)

Matching resources appear in a Results section above the groups, with up to three rows and a line that says how many more there are. Resources with a count other than zero come first, in alphabetical order, and resources with a count of zero follow when the search options allow them. Slots in your groups that match are highlighted as well; a pool slot matches by its pool name. Click the X next to the field, or press Escape while typing, to clear the search.

Search options let you hide zero-count results or remove the field from the header (see [Search options](topic:search-options)). The counts follow the count options on the Options tab (see [Count options](topic:count-options)).
