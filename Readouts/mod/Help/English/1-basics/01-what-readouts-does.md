---
title: What Readouts does
---
Readouts replaces the resource readout in the top left corner of the screen with groups of resources that you arrange yourself:

![The readout panel](panel-overview.png)

Each group is a band with a colored stripe. A band shows resource icons with their counts, and a group can hold up to three tiers of resources that you reveal when you need them (see [Showing more tiers](topic:showing-more-tiers)).

The gear button at the top of the panel opens the editor, where you build groups and set options (see [The editor window](topic:the-editor-window)).
