---
title: Reading a band
---
Each group you have turned on is one band in the panel. From left to right, a band shows the colored stripe, the triangles, and one icon with a counter per resource or resource pool:

![One band of the readout](band-parts.png)

- The colored stripe identifies the group. The six colors follow the order of the groups you have turned on and repeat after that, so hiding a group shifts the colors of the bands below it.
- The triangles, one per tier the group has. A white triangle marks a tier that is shown, a dim one a tier that is hidden.
- One icon and counter per resource or resource pool (see [What a resource pool is](topic:what-a-resource-pool-is)).

Counts above ten thousand are shortened, so 12,800 reads 12.8k. The tooltip shows the exact number (see [Hovering and clicking a slot](topic:hovering-and-clicking-a-slot)).

A counter changes color when the count falls to a limit you set for that resource (see [Slot options](topic:slot-options)). It also turns orange when it shows a negative number, which happens when your bills and blueprints need more than you have (see [Planned work](topic:planned-work)).
