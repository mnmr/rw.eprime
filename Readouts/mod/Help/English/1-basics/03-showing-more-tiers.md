---
title: Showing more tiers
---
<!-- l10n:b-059c4e6aaebe -->
A group can hold up to three tiers. The first tier is always shown; the others stay hidden until you reveal them. The Food group with only its first tier:

<!-- l10n:b-eed8f74b2898 -->
![Food with one tier](food-depth-1.png)

<!-- l10n:b-4e1b9bdaaae0 -->
Click the stripe or the triangles to reveal the next tier. Each click adds one tier, and a click on the last tier goes back to the first. Hovering the stripe shows a tooltip that says so:

<!-- l10n:b-175b436fb118 -->
![The tooltip on the stripe](rail-tooltip.png)

<!-- l10n:b-fe696b913a68 -->
One click adds the second tier to the right of the first, and another click adds the third:

<!-- l10n:b-c62512ad5a42 -->
![Food with two tiers](food-depth-2.png)

<!-- l10n:b-38d8c5c82f43 -->
![Food with all three tiers](food-depth-3.png)

<!-- l10n:b-8e05654445fc -->
With the vertical layout the extra tiers go below the first one instead (see [Tier layout](topic:tier-layout)). Hovering a band can also reveal its tiers for as long as the mouse stays on it (see [Hover options](topic:hover-options)).

<!-- l10n:b-c1f0560bf7bf -->
Which tiers you have open is your own view. Other players in a multiplayer game keep theirs.
