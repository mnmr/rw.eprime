---
title: Editing a pool
---
<!-- l10n:b-9a90b7ce7ee4 -->
Switch the middle panel to the Resource Pool Editor with the button at its top:

<!-- l10n:b-bbf0bf6b9065 -->
![The switch between the two editors](editor-mode.png)

<!-- l10n:b-44b1617ac909 -->
Click Add at the bottom and enter a name to create a pool, then click a pool in the list to edit it. The two buttons on a row rename or delete the pool.

<!-- l10n:b-de5368281721 -->
With a pool selected, the Resources panel shows a checkbox in front of every category and resource:

<!-- l10n:b-34510d73d777 -->
![Members of the Meals pool in the Resources panel](pool-members.png)

<!-- l10n:b-4fffedd19842 -->
Click the checkbox of a category to include everything in it, or the checkbox of single resources. A category with only some of its members in the pool shows a partial mark, and clicking a category name opens or closes it. A category checkbox only changes the resources currently listed under it, so an active search, item, or source filter limits what it adds or removes.

<!-- l10n:b-d469974a5c90 -->
Click the icon of any resource in the list to make it the pool's icon. The name of the resource you chose as the icon is shown in yellow; the automatic first-member icon is not marked.
