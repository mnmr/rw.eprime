<!-- l10n:summary -->
Toggleable Readouts, including the Continued version, replaces how the
vanilla readout is drawn.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
While EPrime's Readouts is enabled the vanilla readout is not drawn, so its
filtering and pinning never apply.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. In EPrime's Readouts, a slot's
[Show when zero](topic:slot-options) option and the tiers you keep open play
the same role as filtering and pinning.
