<!-- l10n:summary -->
Custom Resource Readout draws its own resource panel in its custom and
preset modes.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
That panel and EPrime's Readouts compete for the same corner of the screen,
so both can end up drawn over each other.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. EPrime's Readouts covers the same ground with groups,
tiers and resource pools.
