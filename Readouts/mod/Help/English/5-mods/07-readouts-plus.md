<!-- l10n:summary -->
Readouts+ adds click controls to the vanilla resource readout.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
While EPrime's Readouts is enabled the vanilla readout is not drawn, so those
controls never appear and the mod does nothing.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Clicking a slot in EPrime's Readouts already selects or
cycles through the matching items (see
[Hovering and clicking a slot](topic:hovering-and-clicking-a-slot)).
