Planned work can allow for quality retries.

<!-- l10n:planned-work -->
The **Quality Jobs support** row under [Planned work](topic:planned-work)
adjusts bill and buildable reservations for the quality targets Quality Jobs
manages: it estimates materials for repeat attempts and accounts for the
materials a deconstructed building gives back. The row is greyed out when
Quality Jobs is not installed or is too old, and the reason shows on hover.
