---
title: The toolbar toggle
---
<!-- l10n:b-1d46aed8d43f -->
The game has a resource readout button in the toolbar at the bottom right of the screen. Readouts hides it, because the panel has its own way of showing tiers.

<!-- l10n:b-139023e744e6 -->
Keep the readout toggle in the toolbar brings the button back with a new purpose: clicking it shows or hides the groups. With the groups hidden, the panel keeps only the gear and the search field, and a search still shows its results.

<!-- l10n:b-3cbc0d291c2d -->
The button uses the game's own setting for the categorized readout, so if you switch back to the game's readout later, that setting is whatever you last clicked.
