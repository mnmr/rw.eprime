Everything on the Options tab is your own setting. It applies to all your saves and never changes what other players see.

<!-- l10n:b-348e3f401472 -->
Tier layout decides where a group puts its extra tiers when you reveal them, whether by clicking the stripe or by hovering. Horizontal adds the tiers to the right, on the same row:

<!-- l10n:b-768d7af0f673 -->
![Horizontal layout: the Drugs group with two tiers](tiers-horizontal.png)

<!-- l10n:b-71c01052c3d7 -->
Vertical gives each tier its own row and makes the band taller:
