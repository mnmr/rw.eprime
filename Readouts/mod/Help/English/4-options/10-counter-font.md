---
title: Counter font
---
<!-- l10n:b-bbafef0937b0 -->
Counter font size is in Display Options on the Options tab. Choose Tiny or Small. Tiny is the default.

<!-- l10n:b-28aebe2a60b7 -->
![The counter font option in Display Options](counter-font.png)

<!-- l10n:b-7ef0926a6d97 -->
Both sizes override the game's tiny-text setting. Changes apply immediately; resource counts stay the same.

<!-- l10n:b-a58e330421aa -->
You can reach these options from the gear or from Mod Options (see [Panel position and size](topic:panel-position-and-size)).
