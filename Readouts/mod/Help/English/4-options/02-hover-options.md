---
title: Hover options
---
Expand on hover reveals every tier of a band while the mouse is over it. Tiers added by hovering show yellow triangles, so you can still see which tiers you opened yourself:

![The hovered Food group: the first tier is yours, the other two came from hovering](hover-expanded.png)

Collapse when not hovering appears once Expand on hover is on, and hides instead of reveals. Every band shrinks to its stripe and triangles until you hover it:

![Two collapsed bands](collapse-idle.png)

A hovered band shows the tiers you opened, never more:

![The hovered Drugs band with its two tiers](collapse-hover.png)

Both options follow the tier layout you chose (see [Tier layout](topic:tier-layout)).
