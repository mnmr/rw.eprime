## Position and size

<!-- l10n:b-15c2f6d16365 -->
The sliders under Position and Size Options adjust the panel:

<!-- l10n:b-b666a5f1345e -->
![The panel position and size sliders](position-size.png)

<!-- l10n:b-67c2c540ddd4 -->
- Horizontal offset and Vertical offset move the panel away from the corner, from 0 to 200 pixels. Both start at 7.
- Panel width sets the width of the header and of the search results, from 80 to 400 pixels with 140 as the default. Wide bands extend past it, and a wider panel fits more search-result columns, up to six.
- Bottom margin keeps the panel clear of the bottom of the screen, from 0 to 500 pixels with 200 as the default. A panel that would reach past it scrolls with the mouse wheel instead.

<!-- l10n:b-7b42232657ab -->
Changes preview immediately. Reset position and size restores these four defaults without changing your other options.

<!-- l10n:b-27b9e8c99811 -->
## Use the game's readout

<!-- l10n:b-48ae8ebe7450 -->
Use vanilla readout, at the bottom of Display Options, asks for confirmation before switching to the game's readout and its categorized-readout toggle. You can keep Readouts installed.

<!-- l10n:b-a1b50e1767fc -->
To bring Readouts back after closing the settings window, open Options → Mod Options → EPrime's Readouts and turn off Use vanilla readout.
