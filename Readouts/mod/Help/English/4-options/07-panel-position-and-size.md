---
title: Panel position and size
---
The position and width of the panel are set in the game's own mod settings. Open the Options menu, choose Mod settings, and pick EPrime's Readouts.

- Use vanilla readout switches back to the game's readout, including its categorized-readout toggle, without removing the mod.
- Horizontal offset and Vertical offset move the panel away from the corner, from 0 to 200 pixels. Both start at 7.
- Panel width sets the width of the header and of the search results, from 80 to 400 pixels with 140 as the default. Wide bands extend past it, and a wider panel fits more search-result columns, up to six.
- Bottom margin keeps the panel clear of the bottom of the screen, from 0 to 500 pixels with 200 as the default. A panel that would reach past it scrolls with the mouse wheel instead.
