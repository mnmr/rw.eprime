---
title: Retrying construction
---
<!-- l10n:loop -->
A building below its **{k:QJ_MinQualityLabel}** is marked for deconstruction, then rebuilt at the same place. This repeats until it reaches the target or you stop management. **{k:QJ_NoRetries}** keeps the first result while still applying your finisher requirements.

<!-- l10n:cost -->
Retries use labor and materials, and some materials are lost each time. The building is unavailable while it is being replaced. Good finisher requirements reduce wasted attempts; a very ambitious target can take many tries.

<!-- l10n:retained -->
Replacements keep material, orientation, style and paint. Beds also keep medical and prisoner settings. Original bed owners return when the loop ends if they are still eligible and have not taken another bed. Other mods' custom building settings follow their usual deconstruct-and-rebuild behavior.

<!-- l10n:stop -->
Use **{k:QJ_Clear}**, toggle off **{k:QJ_GizmoQualityJobLabel}**, or cancel the deconstruction order to stop the loop. If the original has already been demolished, Clear lets the replacement finish normally while retaining its saved settings.
