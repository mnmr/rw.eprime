---
title: New buildings
---
<!-- l10n:enable -->
Select a blueprint or frame for a building with quality, such as a bed or chair. **Left-click Quality Job** to toggle management using your construction defaults. **Right-click** it to choose the finisher requirements and target quality.

<!-- l10n:construction-image -->
![Quality Job controls for a new building](construction.png)

<!-- l10n:settings -->
Changing a setting in this panel enables management. **{k:QJ_Clear}** removes it and lets the build finish normally. Select several eligible blueprints or frames to apply a change to all of them.

<!-- l10n:finish -->
Managed construction has a sparkle overlay. Builders can do the early work, but a frame waits at the end until a qualifying builder can finish. If the current builder already qualifies, construction finishes without a handoff.

<!-- l10n:copy -->
The normal **Build copy** command carries a managed blueprint or frame's Quality Jobs settings to the new blueprints. To manage all new quality buildings automatically, enable **{k:QJ_SettingsManageNewConstruction}** in Options.

<!-- l10n:retry-link -->
Leave **{k:QJ_MinQualityLabel}** at **{k:QJ_NoRetries}** to accept the first result. Choose a quality to [rebuild below-target results](topic:retries).
