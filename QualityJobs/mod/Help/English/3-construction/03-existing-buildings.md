---
title: Improving existing buildings
---
<!-- l10n:open -->
Select one or more completed quality buildings and click **{k:QJ_GizmoQualityJobLabel}**. Choose a target quality and builder requirements, then click **{k:QJ_StartRebuilding}**. Opening the panel or changing its settings alone does not demolish anything.

<!-- l10n:rebuild-image -->
![The completed-building panel with Start rebuilding](rebuild.png)

<!-- l10n:eligible -->
Buildings already meeting the target are kept. A building must be available to construct and free of other deconstruction or reinstall orders. If none of the selected buildings need an eligible upgrade, the panel tells you so.

<!-- l10n:consequence -->
Starting uses the same [deconstruct-and-rebuild loop](topic:retries) as new construction. Plan for the material cost and temporary loss of beds or furniture. Clear management or cancel deconstruction if you change your mind.
