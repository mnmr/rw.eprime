title: Other mods and multiplayer
---
<!-- l10n:requirements -->
Quality Jobs requires Harmony and supports RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. No DLC is required. Ideology adds the production-specialist requirement; ordinary quality management works without it.

<!-- l10n:expanded -->
**Vanilla Skills Expanded:** construction, crafting and art quality bonuses count in finisher selection and the odds preview. Speed bonuses do not give a quality preference. Changes to these bonuses may need a little simulation time to appear.

<!-- l10n:ingredients -->
**Quality Bionics Remastered:** supported quality-producing recipes can use the bill panel. **Vanilla Genetics Expanded:** recipes whose quality comes from a genoframe keep that ingredient's quality rules; choosing a finisher cannot improve the ingredient's quality.

<!-- l10n:implanner -->
**Implanner** can request quality-managed implant bills. **WorkRoles** can organize the finishing work through work assignments; the finished item's work type must still be enabled for the pawn.

<!-- l10n:conflicts -->
Avoid overlapping unfinished-work managers. The welcome notice identifies detected conflicts, including **No Job Authors** and **Finish it!**. They can bypass reserved work or expect an unfinished item to remain attached to its original bill. Turning sharing off does not remove every conflict.

<!-- l10n:multiplayer -->
Colony settings and quality-work commands are synchronized in Multiplayer. Toolbar visibility and which help topics you have read are personal preferences.
