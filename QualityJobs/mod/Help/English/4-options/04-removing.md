---
title: Disabling or removing the mod
---
<!-- l10n:individual -->
For just one bill, clear **{k:QJ_ManageBill}**. For a construction plan, use **{k:QJ_Clear}** or toggle **{k:QJ_GizmoQualityJobLabel}** off. Use the global Disable button only when you want to discard Quality Jobs configuration for the whole save.

<!-- l10n:remove-steps -->
1. Let replacement buildings finish first, so their saved bed settings and paint are restored.
2. Open Options and click **{k:QJ_DisableButton}**, then confirm.
3. Save the game. Remove the mod before loading that save again.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
Disabling restores set-aside items to vanilla ownership and discards the save's bill conditions, construction plans and stock caps. Removing the mod without disabling first can leave unfinished items without an owner; those items must be cancelled.

<!-- l10n:reload -->
If you keep Quality Jobs installed, loading the save activates it again with default settings. Disabling is a preparation for removal, not a lasting per-save off switch.
