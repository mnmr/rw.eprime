---
title: When work waits
---
<!-- l10n:check -->
Start with **{k:QJ_StatusHeader}** in the bill or construction panel. **No eligible finisher** means the work is waiting for your requirements to be met, not that its progress was lost.

<!-- l10n:checklist -->
- Is the required work type enabled for the intended finisher, and are they on this map and able to work?
- Is the skill requirement too high, or are you waiting for Inspired Creativity or a production specialist?
- Is Auto-adjust waiting for a worker with higher expected quality to become available, for example to return from a caravan or finish recharging?
- Can the worker reach and reserve the item or frame, and use the bench? Check forbidden items, allowed areas, bill restrictions and competing reservations.
- Is the stock cap full? Finish waiting items before starting more, or deliberately raise the cap.

<!-- l10n:sharing-wait -->
**This bill's unfinished item is waiting to be resumed** prevents a duplicate while shared work is already waiting or being hauled. Let that item become available rather than supplying another set of ingredients.

<!-- l10n:refresh -->
Most setting changes apply immediately, even while paused. Some changing pawn facts and optional-mod bonuses refresh after simulation advances; if a recent change is not reflected yet, resume briefly. High-priority finishing waits until the pawn next looks for work.
