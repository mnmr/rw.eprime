title: Getting started
---
<!-- l10n:purpose -->
Quality Jobs lets your colony share the work while a qualified worker does the finishing touch. Colonists, colony mechs and supported robots can finish work they are capable of doing. In RimWorld, the **finisher** determines quality. The mod chooses who finishes; the result is still a normal quality roll.

<!-- l10n:toolbar -->
Click the Quality Jobs icon beside the view toggles in the lower right. **{k:QJ_TabOptions}** holds your defaults; **{k:QJ_TabHelp}** explains the controls. Choose a chapter above and a topic on the left.

<!-- l10n:toolbar-image -->
![The Quality Jobs toolbar icon](toolbar.png)

<!-- l10n:first-steps -->
- For gear and art, open a workbench bill's **Details** and use its **{k:QJ_QualityPanelTitle}** panel.
- For a new building with quality, select its blueprint or frame and use **{k:QJ_GizmoQualityJobLabel}**.
- To improve existing furniture, select it, choose a target in **{k:QJ_GizmoQualityJobLabel}**, then click **{k:QJ_StartRebuilding}**.

<!-- l10n:defaults -->
New quality bills are managed by default. New buildings are opt-in unless you enable **{k:QJ_SettingsManageNewConstruction}**. When adding the mod to a save, the existing-bills prompt lets you keep their current behavior or enable automatic selection of the best finisher.
