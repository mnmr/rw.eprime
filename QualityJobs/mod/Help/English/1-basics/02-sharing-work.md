---
title: Sharing unfinished work
---
<!-- l10n:sharing -->
With **{k:QJ_SettingsShareWork}** enabled in Options, an idle unfinished item can be continued by another eligible worker, even if its original crafter has died. Sharing works independently of **{k:QJ_ManageBill}**.

<!-- l10n:matching -->
The next worker needs a usable bill for the same recipe. The item's ingredients must be allowed by that bill, and its style must match. The item must also be reachable, allowed and available to reserve. Sharing does not bypass ordinary work assignments or bill restrictions.

<!-- l10n:handoff -->
An idle item is released from its original worker and bill so a compatible bill can pick it up. While that bill's unfinished item is waiting or being hauled, it does not start a duplicate. Active work is not interrupted just to hand it to someone else.

<!-- l10n:reserved -->
Sharing early work is different from reserving the final touch. A managed item paused just before completion shows **Author: reserved (Quality Jobs)**. It waits for a [qualifying finisher](topic:finishers); an ordinary worker cannot finish it through sharing.

<!-- l10n:robot-work -->
When **{k:QJ_SettingsAllowRobotPreparation}** is off, a shared unfinished item remains reserved for colonist work while its original bill still exists and is managed. Picking it up through an unmanaged bill does not let a mech or robot take over that work. Eligible mechs and robots can still perform finishing assignments from Quality Jobs.

<!-- l10n:off -->
Turn sharing off to keep ordinary unfinished work with its original crafter. Managed bills can still reserve the final touch and hand it to a qualifying finisher. Turning off **{k:QJ_ManageBill}** removes that bill's finishing requirements and releases its quality-waiting work; it does not turn off sharing for the colony.
