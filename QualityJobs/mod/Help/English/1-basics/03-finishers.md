---
title: Choosing finishers
---
<!-- l10n:requirements -->
Use these controls on a bill or a building. Other eligible workers may do the earlier work; only the finishing touch must meet your requirements.

<!-- l10n:controls -->
- **Finisher skill** sets a minimum skill level.
- **{k:QJ_AutoBest}** chooses the best eligible colonist automatically. The manual slider is kept for when you turn this off.
- **{k:QJ_RequireInspired}** waits for that inspiration. Completing the work consumes it as usual.
- **{k:QJ_RequireSpecialist}** requires an Ideology role that grants a production quality bonus. This option appears with Ideology.

![Finisher requirements and the quality-odds preview](finisher-options.png)

<!-- l10n:best -->
Auto-adjust compares the colony's eligible colonists, including those temporarily away, downed or busy. It may therefore wait for your best worker rather than settle for someone weaker. Quality bonuses count when choosing the best finisher.

<!-- l10n:odds -->
**{k:QJ_OddsHeader}** compares your **{k:QJ_OddsColConfig}** with the current **{k:QJ_OddsColBest}** candidate. These are chances, not promises. **Eligible finishers** lists colonists who can qualify on this map now; away, downed or mentally broken colonists are excluded from that list.

<!-- l10n:priority -->
**{k:QJ_SettingsHighPriorityFinish}** makes assigned finishing work come before other work except firefighting, medical care, rescue and bed rest. It does not interrupt the current job. With it off, finishing comes first within its work type, after any higher-ranked work types. The item's normal work type must be enabled.
