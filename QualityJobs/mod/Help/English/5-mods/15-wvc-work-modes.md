<!-- l10n:summary -->
A mech in any of its work modes qualifies as a finisher.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Mechs qualify in any work mode that actually runs work, so the work modes
WVC adds keep a mech available. Its combat, escort and recharge modes make a
mech unavailable.
