<!-- l10n:summary -->
WorkRoles can organize the finishing work through work assignments.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Role assignments decide who is available to finish; the finished item's
work type must still be enabled for the colonist. With WorkRoles' robot and
mech support on, its mechanical recommendations follow the
**{k:QJ_SettingsAllowRobotPreparation}** option.
