<!-- l10n:summary -->
Robots that can do the work qualify as finishers.

<!-- l10n:robots -->
Colony-controlled workers that can perform the required work can qualify as
finishers, ranked by expected quality alongside colonists. Robots keep their
own work capabilities; a hauling or cleaning robot cannot finish crafting or
construction. **{k:QJ_SettingsAllowRobotPreparation}** controls whether mechs
and supported robots may also do the earlier work on managed jobs. Turning it
off keeps them available for finishing.

<!-- l10n:charging -->
A robot or mech that is charging does not block others from working on the
bill.
