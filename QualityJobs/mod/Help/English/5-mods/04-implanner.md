<!-- l10n:summary -->
Implanner can request quality-managed implant bills.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
When a plan in Implanner sets a minimum implant quality, the bills it
creates are managed by Quality Jobs at that target, so the bench keeps
trying until an implant of that quality or better comes out.
