<!-- l10n:summary -->
The Finish jobs do not appear in its categories.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
Personal Work Categories collects jobs before Quality Jobs registers its
Finish jobs, so they cannot be moved into a category of their own. The
finisher still does the work at high priority through the Quality Jobs
option for it; only the placement in that mod's grid is missing.
