<!-- l10n:summary -->
Finish it! expects an unfinished item to stay attached to its bill.

<!-- l10n:effect -->
It can fail when used on unfinished work that Quality Jobs has detached
from its bill for a finisher.

<!-- l10n:advice -->
Keep one of the two. Quality Jobs' own finisher selection already sends
unfinished work to the colonist most likely to reach the target.
