<!-- l10n:summary -->
The dye and style chosen on a bill survive shared work.

<!-- l10n:detail -->
When a finisher completes work another colonist started, the item keeps the
colour and style selected through Craft with Color. The Basic style stays
unstyled even when the finisher's ideology supplies a style, and the Random
style picks a style as each item finishes instead of repeating one saved
choice.
